OnePlus Pad 4 · SM8850P “iceland” · mainline Linux + Fedora 44

Pad 4 feature matrix

Everything is tested by booting from RAM; nothing is installed on the tablet yet. Status as of 2 Oct 2026: Wi-Fi finds 5 GHz networks at boot (the firmware is now told not to use a two-radio mode this tablet's hardware cannot do, as stock does); both cameras deliver first raw pictures; hardware video decoding is complete, 10-bit included, and GNOME Web uses it; the About page shows "OnePlus Pad 4" and the processor; GNOME's three power modes switch tablet profiles. In progress: battery time estimate, sleep, auto-rotate, camera exposure. Boot & platform waits for your go-ahead on the install.
