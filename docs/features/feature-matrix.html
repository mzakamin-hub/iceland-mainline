Pad 4 feature matrix

Everything is tested by booting from RAM; nothing is installed on the tablet yet. Status as of 30 Sep 2026: the GPU passes the conformance tests (the few failures are Mesa gaps for this new GPU, not the kernel), WebGL runs on the GPU, DisplayPort over USB-C comes up, sensors, refresh-rate changes, the pink glitches and the microphone hiss are fixed; battery and thermal protection in place. Now: hardware video decoding. Next: the NPU; in parallel the cameras, Wi-Fi 5 GHz and USB accessories (those need you plugging things in). Boot & platform waits for your go-ahead on the install.
