OnePlus Pad 4 · SM8850P “iceland” · mainline Linux + Fedora 44

Pad 4 feature matrix

Everything is tested by booting from RAM; nothing is installed on the tablet yet. Status as of 2 Oct 2026: charging follows stock's steps and limits, the charge limit (80 / 85 %) and low-battery limiting are kernel features, GPU cooling is proven level by level, and two kernel thermal faults are fixed: CPU limits that stayed after a heavy load, and PMIC cooling maps that never took effect. Auto-rotate, both cameras with automatic exposure, 5 GHz Wi-Fi, hardware video, the three power modes and sleep work. In progress: the cameras (why their bus drops out is found in stock's sources and under test; focus; the Camera app), wall-charger tests with you, and the choice of the temperature policy. The plan for the install is written (docs/INSTALL-PLAN.md); the install itself waits for your go-ahead.
